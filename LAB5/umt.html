Welcome to Universiti Malaysia Terengganu, a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. UMT is recognised for its focus on marine science, environmental studies, technology and sustainable development.

About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

History of UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) began as the Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science under Universiti Pertanian Malaysia. It was established to provide facilities for students to conduct practical activities and for lecturers to carry out research. In June 1996, the centre became a branch campus known as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT).

In 1999, the establishment of Kolej Universiti Terengganu (KUT) was approved. The institution was subsequently renamed Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) in 2001. On 1 February 2007, KUSTEM was upgraded to full university status and officially became Universiti Malaysia Terengganu.

Today, UMT focuses on education, research and innovation, particularly in marine science, fisheries, aquaculture and environmental sustainability. Its strategic location near the South China Sea supports research and learning related to marine resources and the environment.
