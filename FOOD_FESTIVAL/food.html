About the Festival

Welcome to the Taste of Terengganu Food Festival! This special event celebrates the delicious traditional foods and unique culinary heritage of Terengganu. Visitors can enjoy a variety of local dishes prepared by talented food vendors from different parts of the state.

The festival provides an opportunity for visitors to experience authentic Terengganu flavours, discover traditional recipes and support local businesses. From the famous Nasi Dagang to Keropok Lekor, everyone can enjoy the wonderful taste of local food while spending quality time with family and friends.

Free Entry! Everyone is welcome. Bring your family and friends to enjoy delicious traditional Terengganu food.
